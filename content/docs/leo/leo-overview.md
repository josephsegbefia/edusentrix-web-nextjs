# Leo AI Assistant

Leo is EduSentrix's built-in AI assistant, powered by OpenAI. Leo helps teachers, administrators, and staff work faster by generating content, summarising data, and offering intelligent suggestions — all without leaving the platform.

## What Is Leo?

Leo is an AI-powered tool integrated across several modules of EduSentrix. Think of Leo as a knowledgeable assistant who can draft lesson notes, suggest exam questions, summarise student data, and more.

- Leo is available wherever you see the **Leo icon** — look for it in Lesson Notes, Exams, Reports, and Finance.
- Everything Leo generates is a **starting point** — you always review, edit, and approve before using it.
- Leo learns from the context you give it (your subject, class level, curriculum) to produce relevant results.

> **Tip:** The more specific your instructions to Leo, the better the output. Instead of "Write a lesson note," try "Write a 40-minute lesson note on fractions for Primary 5 using the NaCCA curriculum."

## Lesson Note Generation

One of Leo's most powerful features is generating full lesson notes.

- Open a lesson note and click **Generate with Leo**.
- Leo creates a complete lesson plan including objectives, introduction, activities, assessment, and conclusion.
- **Expand sections** — highlight a section and ask Leo to add more detail, examples, or differentiated activities.
- **Suggest activities** — ask Leo to recommend hands-on activities, group work ideas, or discussion prompts.
- Edit the generated content freely — it's your lesson, Leo just gives you a head start.

## Exam Question Drafting

Save hours of exam preparation with AI-generated questions.

- From the **Question Bank** or exam creation screen, click **Generate Questions with Leo**.
- Specify the subject, topic, difficulty level, and question format (multiple choice, short answer, essay).
- Leo generates a set of questions aligned to your specifications.
- Review and refine each question before adding it to your exam.

> **Tip:** Generate more questions than you need, then pick the best ones. Leo gives you variety to choose from.

## Student Insight Summaries

Get a quick understanding of how a student is performing without sifting through raw data.

- Navigate to a student's profile and click **Leo Insights**.
- Leo summarises the student's academic performance, attendance trends, and behavioural notes into a concise overview.
- Use these summaries for parent-teacher conferences or internal reviews.

## Financial Brief Generation

Administrators can use Leo to make sense of financial data.

- From the **Finance** module, ask Leo to generate a **financial brief**.
- Leo summarises revenue, outstanding fees, payment trends, and key highlights for a selected period.
- Use these briefs for board meetings, term reviews, or quick status checks.

## Scheme of Work Planning

Leo can help teachers plan out an entire term's scheme of work.

- When creating a scheme of work, click **Plan with Leo**.
- Leo suggests a week-by-week breakdown of topics based on your selected curriculum and subject.
- Adjust the plan as needed — reorder topics, add extra weeks, or modify the pacing.

## Tips for Getting the Best Results from Leo

- **Be specific** — include the subject, class level, curriculum, and any special requirements.
- **Iterate** — if the first output isn't perfect, refine your prompt and try again.
- **Use context** — Leo works best when it knows what you're working on. Use it within the relevant module rather than asking generic questions.
- **Review everything** — Leo is a tool, not a replacement. Always check generated content for accuracy and appropriateness.
- **Experiment** — try different prompts to discover what works best for your teaching style.

> **Tip:** Leo improves the more context it has. If you're working within a curriculum-linked subject, Leo will automatically align its suggestions to the relevant standards.
