# Student Portal

The Student Portal gives students access to their academic information, assignments, and school announcements. Access it at `/student` after logging in with a student account.

## Overview

The Student Portal provides:
- **Dashboard**: Personal academic overview
- **Assignments**: View and submit assignments
- **Results**: Access academic results and grades
- **Calendar**: See school events and deadlines
- **Notices**: Read school-wide announcements
- **Profile**: Manage personal information

## Dashboard

The student dashboard (`/student`) displays:
- Current academic term information
- Recent grades and assessment scores
- Upcoming assignments and deadlines
- School announcements

## Assignments

### Viewing Assignments

Navigate to `/student/assignments`:
- See all assigned work from your teachers
- Filter by subject or status (pending, submitted, graded)
- View due dates and point values

### Submitting Assignments

1. Click on an assignment to view details at `/student/assignments/[id]`
2. Read the instructions and any attached resources
3. Upload your submission file(s)
4. Add any notes or comments
5. Submit before the deadline

### Tracking Grades

After an assignment is graded:
- View your score and feedback
- See the rubric breakdown (if applicable)
- Compare with the total possible points

## Results

View your academic results at `/student/results`:
- Term-by-term grade summary
- Subject-wise performance breakdown
- CA scores and exam scores
- Class position (if available)
- Grade scale interpretation

## Calendar

Access the academic calendar at `/student/calendar`:
- School events and holidays
- Exam schedules
- Assignment deadlines
- Important dates

## Notices

Read school announcements at `/student/notices`:
- School-wide notices from administrators
- Class-specific notices from teachers
- Important updates and reminders

## Profile

Manage your information at `/student/profile`:
- View personal details
- Update contact information (where permitted)
- See class and grade assignment

## Tips for Students

1. **Check Daily**: Log in each day to see new assignments and notices
2. **Submit Early**: Don't wait until the last minute
3. **Review Results**: Check your grades regularly and reach out to teachers if you have questions
4. **Use the Calendar**: Keep track of exam dates and deadlines
5. **Read Notices**: Stay informed about school events
