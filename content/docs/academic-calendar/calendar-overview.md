# Academic Calendar

The Academic Calendar is your school's single source of truth for every important date — from exam weeks and holidays to PTA meetings and inter-house sports. Everyone sees the events that matter to them, and Leo AI helps you carry recurring events forward each term.

## Creating an Event

1. Go to **Calendar** in the sidebar and click **"New Event"**.
2. Enter the **title, description, date(s), and time** (or mark it as an all-day event).
3. Choose an **event type** (see below).
4. Set the **audience** — who should see this event.
5. Click **Save** or **Publish** to make it visible.

> **Tip:** You can also create an event by clicking directly on a date in the calendar view. EduSentrix will pre-fill the date for you.

## Event Types

Colour-coded event types make the calendar easy to scan at a glance:

- **Academic** — Lessons, curriculum deadlines, open days
- **Examination** — Mid-terms, end-of-term exams, mock exams
- **Holiday** — Public holidays, school vacations, half-term breaks
- **Sports** — Inter-house competitions, athletics days, fixtures
- **Meeting** — Staff meetings, PTA meetings, board sessions
- **Activity** — Club meetings, excursions, cultural events
- **Non-Teaching Day** — INSET days, teacher professional development

Each type has a distinct colour, so a quick glance at the calendar tells you what kind of week is ahead.

## Recurring Events

Many school events repeat on a schedule. When creating an event, toggle **"Recurring"** and choose a pattern:

- **Daily** — Every day or every weekday
- **Weekly** — Same day each week (e.g., staff meeting every Monday)
- **Bi-weekly** — Every two weeks
- **Monthly** — Same date or same weekday each month

Set an **end date** or a **number of occurrences** to control how long the series runs. You can later edit a single occurrence or the entire series.

## Audience Targeting

Not every event is relevant to everyone. EduSentrix lets you scope visibility:

- **School-wide** — Visible to all staff, students, and parents
- **By Grade** — Only users in selected grades (e.g., "JHS 3 Mock Exams")
- **By Class** — Specific class groups (e.g., "Class 6A Field Trip")
- **Specific Users** — Hand-picked individuals (e.g., "Debate Team Members")

> **Tip:** Parents automatically see events that target their ward's grade or class, so you don't need to add them separately.

## Reminders

Each event can have one or more reminders:

- **In-app notification** — Appears in the notification bell
- **Email reminder** — Sent to all targeted users
- **Timing** — Choose from 1 hour before, 1 day before, 3 days before, or 1 week before

Reminders are especially useful for submission deadlines, exam dates, and parent events.

## Period-Aware Filtering

Your calendar respects the active **academic period**. Switch between terms or semesters using the period selector at the top of the calendar, and only events within that period are shown. This keeps the view focused and prevents clutter from past or future terms.

## Leo Carryover Suggestions

At the start of a new term, Leo AI reviews your previous term's calendar and suggests events to carry over:

- Recurring meetings and assemblies
- Standing sports fixtures
- Annual celebrations and open days

Leo presents a checklist of suggested events with proposed dates adjusted to the new term. Accept, modify, or dismiss each suggestion with a single click.

> **Tip:** Leo also flags potential date conflicts — for example, if a suggested PTA meeting falls on a public holiday in the new term.

## Publishing & Visibility

Events can be saved as **Draft** or **Published**:

- **Draft** events are only visible to the creator and admins. Use drafts to plan tentatively before making events official.
- **Published** events are visible to the targeted audience immediately.

You can unpublish an event at any time if plans change, and it will disappear from everyone's calendar without being deleted.

## Calendar Views

EduSentrix offers multiple views to suit different planning needs:

- **Month view** — The classic overview for long-range planning
- **Week view** — A detailed look at the current or upcoming week
- **List view** — A chronological list, ideal for printing or quick scanning

## Best Practices

1. **Publish early** — Give parents and teachers as much notice as possible.
2. **Use event types consistently** — Colour coding only helps if everyone uses the same categories.
3. **Review Leo's suggestions** — Spending five minutes at the start of term saves hours of manual re-entry.
4. **Target your audience** — Avoid notification fatigue by scoping events to the people who need them.
5. **Set reminders for high-stakes dates** — Exam dates and fee deadlines deserve a one-week and one-day reminder at minimum.
