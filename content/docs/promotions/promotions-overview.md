# Student Promotions

At the end of every academic year, schools need to move students to the next class. EduSentrix's Promotions module makes this process structured, transparent, and easy to manage — whether you promote students automatically based on results or handle each case manually.

## Creating Promotion Cycles

A promotion cycle represents a single round of student advancement decisions.

- Go to **Promotions → New Cycle** to get started.
- Select the academic year and term the cycle applies to.
- Choose the classes or levels included in this promotion round.
- Give the cycle a descriptive name (e.g. "End of Year 2025/2026 Promotions").

> **Tip:** You can create separate cycles for different sections of your school — for example, one for lower primary and another for upper primary.

## Setting Promotion Policies

Promotion policies define the rules that determine whether a student advances, repeats, or needs special consideration.

- Set **minimum score thresholds** — for example, students must achieve at least 50% overall to be promoted.
- Define criteria per subject or as a cumulative average.
- Add conditions for specific subjects (e.g. "Must pass Mathematics and English").
- Policies can differ by class level, so JHS criteria can be stricter than primary.

## Auto-Assign and Manual Placement

Once your policies are in place, let EduSentrix do the heavy lifting.

- **Auto-assign** runs your promotion policies against every student's results and places them into the appropriate next class.
- Students who meet the criteria are marked **Promoted**; those who don't are flagged for review.
- Use **manual placement** to override any decision — move a student to a different class, hold them back, or handle edge cases.

> **Tip:** Always run auto-assign first, then review the flagged students manually. This saves time while keeping you in control.

## Reviewing and Approving Decisions

Before anything is final, you get a full review screen.

- See every student's promotion status at a glance — promoted, retained, or pending review.
- Filter by class, status, or search for a specific student.
- Click into any student to see their scores, the policy that was applied, and the resulting decision.
- Adjust decisions individually if needed before moving forward.

## Finalizing Promotions

When you're satisfied with all decisions, finalize the cycle.

- Finalizing locks in all promotion decisions and moves students to their new classes.
- Students' class assignments update across the entire platform — attendance, fees, timetables, and more.
- Parents and teachers see the updated class placements immediately.

> **Tip:** Double-check all pending reviews before finalizing. Once finalized, changes require a rollback.

## Rollback Capability

Made a mistake? EduSentrix has you covered.

- If a promotion cycle was finalized in error, administrators can **roll back** the entire cycle.
- Rollback reverses all class assignments and restores students to their previous classes.
- A full audit trail is maintained so you can see what was changed and when.

## Best Practices

- Define your promotion policies at the start of the academic year so the criteria are clear to teachers and parents.
- Run a trial auto-assign before the official round to spot any data issues (missing scores, ungraded subjects).
- Use the review screen to catch edge cases — transfer students, medical exceptions, or students with incomplete records.
