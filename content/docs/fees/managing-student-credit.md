# Managing Student Credit

Student credit represents overpayments or prepayments that can be applied to future invoices. This system ensures accurate financial tracking and provides flexibility for parents.

## What is Student Credit?

Student credit is created when:
- **Overpayment**: Payment exceeds invoice outstanding balance
- **Prepayment**: Payment made before invoice is issued
- **Refund**: Credit issued for adjustments or refunds

Credit is stored as a **formal ledger** with:
- **Balance**: Current available credit amount
- **Entries**: Transaction history (credits and debits)
- **Application History**: Records of credit applied to invoices

## Viewing Student Credit

### From Invoice Detail Page

1. Navigate to an invoice detail page
2. Scroll to **"Student Credit"** section
3. View:
   - Current credit balance
   - Credit transaction history
   - Available credit for application

### Credit Balance Display

- **Available Credit**: Amount that can be applied
- **Total Credit**: All-time credit created
- **Applied Credit**: Credit already used
- **Transaction History**: List of all credit transactions

## Applying Credit to Invoices

### Step 1: Access Credit Application

1. Go to invoice detail page
2. Scroll to **"Student Credit"** section
3. Click **"Apply Credit"** button (if credit available)

### Step 2: Select Amount

- Enter amount to apply
- Cannot exceed:
  - Available credit balance
  - Invoice outstanding balance
- System validates automatically

### Step 3: Apply Credit

1. Review application details
2. Confirm amount
3. Click **"Apply Credit"**
4. Credit reduces invoice balance
5. Credit entry created in ledger

## Credit Transaction Types

### Credit Created
- **Overpayment**: Payment exceeds invoice balance
- **Prepayment**: Payment before invoice issued
- **Adjustment**: Credit from invoice adjustments
- **Manual Entry**: Admin-created credit

### Credit Applied
- **Invoice Payment**: Credit applied to invoice
- **Automatic**: System-applied credit
- **Manual**: Admin-applied credit

## Credit Ledger

The credit ledger tracks all credit transactions:

- **Date**: When transaction occurred
- **Type**: Credit created or applied
- **Amount**: Transaction amount
- **Description**: Reason or reference
- **Balance**: Running balance after transaction

## Best Practices

1. **Monitor Regularly**: Check student credit balances regularly
2. **Apply Promptly**: Apply credit to invoices when available
3. **Document Reasons**: Always document why credit was created
4. **Verify Balances**: Verify credit balances match expectations
5. **Communicate**: Inform parents about credit balances
6. **Reconcile**: Reconcile credit with payment records

## Common Scenarios

### Overpayment Handling

**Scenario**: Parent pays 1,000 GHS but invoice is only 800 GHS

1. Record payment of 1,000 GHS
2. System detects 200 GHS overpayment
3. Creates 200 GHS student credit
4. Invoice marked as paid
5. Credit available for future invoices

### Prepayment Handling

**Scenario**: Parent pays before invoice is issued

1. Record payment without invoice
2. System creates credit balance
3. When invoice is issued, apply credit
4. Credit reduces invoice balance

### Multiple Credit Applications

**Scenario**: Student has credit applied to multiple invoices

1. Credit balance tracked centrally
2. Can be applied to any invoice
3. System prevents double application
4. Ledger shows all applications

## Tips

- **Check Before Creating Invoices**: Check for credit before creating new invoices
- **Apply Automatically**: Consider auto-applying credit when creating invoices
- **Parent Communication**: Inform parents about credit balances
- **Credit Reports**: Generate credit reports for reconciliation
- **Expiration Policy**: Consider credit expiration policies (if applicable)
