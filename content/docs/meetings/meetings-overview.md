# Video Meetings

EduSentrix includes built-in video conferencing so teachers and parents can connect face-to-face without leaving the platform. Whether it's a scheduled parent-teacher conference or a quick check-in, meetings are just a click away.

## Scheduling Meetings

Setting up a meeting is simple and flexible.

- Navigate to **Meetings → Schedule New** to create a meeting.
- Choose the meeting type: **one-on-one** (teacher and a single parent) or **group** (multiple participants).
- Set the date, start time, and expected duration.
- Add a title and optional description so participants know what to expect.
- Invited participants receive a notification with the meeting details and a join link.

> **Tip:** Schedule meetings during hours that work for parents — evenings and weekends are often the most convenient for working families.

## LiveKit-Powered Video Conferencing

EduSentrix uses **LiveKit** to deliver reliable, high-quality video calls.

- Crystal-clear audio and video, optimised for varying internet speeds common across Ghana and Africa.
- Screen sharing for presenting student reports, assignments, or other materials.
- Works directly in your browser — no downloads or plugins required.
- Supports mobile devices, so parents can join from their phones.

## Participant Management

Stay in control of who's in the meeting.

- View a real-time list of all participants currently in the call.
- The meeting host can mute participants, remove disruptive attendees, or lock the meeting to prevent new joiners.
- Add participants after the meeting has started if someone needs to join late.

## Joining Meetings

Joining is straightforward for everyone involved.

- **Teachers** see upcoming meetings on their dashboard and can join with one click.
- **Parents** receive a meeting link via notification (in-app or email) and tap to join.
- A waiting room ensures the host can admit participants when ready.
- Late joiners can enter an ongoing meeting without disrupting the session.

> **Tip:** Test your camera and microphone before the meeting starts. EduSentrix shows a quick preview so you can check everything is working.

## Meeting Notes and Follow-Ups

Keep a record of what was discussed and what comes next.

- During or after a meeting, the host can add **meeting notes** summarising key points.
- Notes are saved alongside the meeting record and can be reviewed later.
- Create **follow-up actions** — for example, "Schedule a remedial session for Kwame in Mathematics."
- Both teachers and parents can reference past meeting notes from the meeting history.

## Meeting History

All past meetings are logged and easy to find.

- Browse previous meetings from **Meetings → History**.
- Filter by date, participant, or meeting type.
- View notes, duration, and attendance for any past meeting.

> **Tip:** Review your meeting history before a new parent conference — it helps you pick up exactly where you left off.

## Best Practices

- Send the meeting link at least a day in advance so parents can prepare.
- Keep meetings focused and concise — 15 to 30 minutes works well for most parent-teacher discussions.
- Always add notes after each meeting to build a useful record over time.
