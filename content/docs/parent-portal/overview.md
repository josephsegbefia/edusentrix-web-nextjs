# Parent Portal

The Parent Portal gives parents and guardians a complete view of their ward's school life. Access it at `/parent` after logging in with a parent account.

## Overview

The Parent Portal provides:
- **Dashboard**: Overview of all your wards at a glance
- **Ward Details**: Detailed information for each child
- **Academics**: View grades, performance, and academic progress
- **Attendance**: Track your ward's attendance record
- **Fees & Payments**: View outstanding fees and payment history
- **Calendar**: See school events and important dates
- **Messages**: Communicate with teachers
- **Notifications**: Stay informed about school activities
- **Reports**: Access academic and financial reports

## Dashboard

The parent dashboard (`/parent`) shows:
- Overview cards for each ward
- Quick stats (grades, attendance, fees)
- Recent activity and notifications
- Quick links to common actions

## Wards

### Ward List

View all your enrolled children at `/parent/wards`:
- Name, class, and grade information
- Current academic status
- Fee status overview

### Ward Details

Click a ward to see detailed information at `/parent/wards/[id]`:
- Personal information
- Academic performance summary
- Recent grades and assessments
- Attendance summary
- Fee balance

## Academics

View academic information at `/parent/academics`:
- Current term grades by subject
- Performance trends over time
- Assessment breakdown (CA scores, exam scores)
- Class position and averages
- Subject-specific performance

## Attendance

Track attendance at `/parent/attendance`:
- Daily attendance records
- Attendance percentage
- Absence reasons and excuses
- Trend over the term

## Fees & Payments

### Viewing Fees

Access fee information at `/parent/fees`:
- Outstanding invoice summary
- Invoice details with line items
- Payment history
- Installment schedules (if applicable)

### Making Payments

Navigate to `/parent/payments`:
- View payable invoices
- Choose payment method (Mobile Money, Card, Bank Transfer)
- Track payment confirmation
- View receipts

## Calendar

View the school calendar at `/parent/calendar`:
- School events and holidays
- Exam schedules
- Parent-teacher meeting dates
- Important deadlines

## Messages

Communicate with teachers at `/parent/messages`:
- Send messages to your ward's teachers
- View message history
- Receive updates and responses

## Notifications

Stay informed at `/parent/notifications`:
- Fee reminders
- Attendance alerts
- Academic updates
- School announcements
- Message notifications

## Reports

Access reports at `/parent/reports`:
- Academic performance reports
- Attendance summaries
- Fee statements

## Tips for Parents

1. **Check Regularly**: Log in regularly to stay updated
2. **Review Grades**: Monitor academic progress each week
3. **Track Attendance**: Follow up on any absences
4. **Pay On Time**: Keep fee payments current
5. **Communicate**: Use messages to reach teachers with concerns
6. **Check Calendar**: Stay aware of upcoming events and deadlines
