# Supply Programs

Supply Programs help schools organise and track the distribution of materials to students — from textbooks and uniforms to stationery and learning aids. Whether it's a government-funded initiative or a school-managed supply list, EduSentrix makes it easy to manage the entire process.

## What Are Supply Programs?

A supply program is a structured plan for distributing a defined set of items to a specific group of students.

- Each program has a name, description, and a list of **line items** (the supplies to be distributed).
- Programs can target all students, specific classes, or individual students.
- Track which students have received their supplies and which are still pending.

> **Tip:** Use supply programs for any distribution — not just school supplies. They work great for distributing exam materials, project kits, or even uniforms.

## Creating a Program

Setting up a new supply program is straightforward.

- Navigate to **Supply Programs → New Program**.
- Enter a program name and description (e.g. "Term 1 Textbook Distribution").
- Add **line items** — each item has a name, quantity per student, and optional notes.
- Set the program's start and end dates.

### Example Line Items

- Mathematics Textbook (Qty: 1)
- Exercise Books (Qty: 5)
- Pencils and Erasers Pack (Qty: 1)
- School Planner (Qty: 1)

## Audience Targeting and Eligibility

Control exactly who receives supplies from each program.

- **All students** — distribute to every enrolled student.
- **By class or level** — target specific classes (e.g. only JHS 1 students).
- **By criteria** — set eligibility rules based on student attributes like boarding status, scholarship recipients, or custom tags.
- Students who don't meet the criteria are automatically excluded.

> **Tip:** If a government program targets only public school students in certain regions, use eligibility rules to match the exact criteria.

## Tracking Distribution Progress

Once a program is active, track every distribution in real time.

- Each student has a checklist showing which items they've received and which are outstanding.
- Teachers or administrators mark items as **distributed** when they hand them out.
- A progress dashboard shows overall completion — how many students have received all items versus how many are pending.
- Filter by class, student, or item to find exactly what you need.

## Who Can Access Supply Programs?

Supply programs are available to different roles with appropriate views.

- **Admins** — full control: create programs, set eligibility, view all progress, and export reports.
- **Teachers** — view programs relevant to their homeroom or subject classes and mark items as distributed.
- **Parents** — see which supplies their child is entitled to and whether they've been received.

## Reporting and Export

Keep records of every distribution.

- Export distribution reports to **CSV** for offline analysis or government reporting.
- View summary statistics — total items distributed, completion rates, and outstanding items.
- Use reports to plan procurement for the next term based on actual distribution data.

> **Tip:** Run a distribution report at the end of each term to reconcile inventory and plan ahead.
