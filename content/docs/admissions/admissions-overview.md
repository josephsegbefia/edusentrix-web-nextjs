# Admissions

Managing student admissions is one of the most important workflows for any school. EduSentrix gives you a complete, end-to-end admissions system — from publishing application forms to enrolling accepted students — all from a single dashboard.

## Creating Admission Cycles

Every admissions process starts with a **cycle**. A cycle represents a specific intake period (e.g. "2026/2027 Academic Year Admissions").

- Navigate to **Admissions → Cycles** and click **New Cycle**.
- Give the cycle a name, set the opening and closing dates, and choose which classes or levels are accepting applications.
- You can run multiple cycles at the same time — for example, one for primary and another for junior high.

> **Tip:** Close a cycle's applications automatically by setting an end date. Parents will see a clear deadline on the portal.

## Setting Up Application Forms

Customise exactly what information you collect from applicants.

- Add fields for student details, guardian contacts, previous school records, medical information, and more.
- Mark fields as required or optional.
- Reorder sections by dragging them into place.
- Preview your form before publishing to make sure it looks right.

## Public Application Portal

EduSentrix generates a **public application link** for each cycle. Share this link with prospective parents so they can apply online from any device.

- Parents fill in the form, upload documents, and submit — no account needed.
- Submitted applications appear instantly in your dashboard for review.
- The portal works beautifully on phones, tablets, and desktops.

## Reviewing and Processing Applications

Once applications come in, your admissions team can review them efficiently.

- View all applications in a filterable list — sort by date, class, status, or custom fields.
- Open any application to see full details, uploaded documents, and internal notes.
- Change an application's status: **Pending → Under Review → Accepted → Enrolled** (or **Rejected**).
- Add internal comments for your team without the parent seeing them.

## Bulk Operations

When you have dozens (or hundreds) of applications, bulk actions save serious time.

- Select multiple applications and change their status in one click.
- Send bulk acceptance or rejection notifications to parents.
- Export a filtered set of applications to CSV for offline review or reporting.

> **Tip:** Use the search and filter bar to narrow down applications before performing bulk actions — this helps avoid mistakes.

## Delegating Admissions Access

Not every staff member needs full admin access. With EduSentrix's delegation system, you can grant specific team members access to the admissions module without exposing other areas of the platform.

- Go to **Settings → Delegations** to assign admissions access to a staff member.
- Delegated staff can review applications, update statuses, and add notes.
- All actions taken by delegated staff are logged for accountability.

## Export and Audit

Keep clean records of every admissions decision.

- Export application data to **CSV** or **PDF** at any time.
- The audit log tracks every status change, who made it, and when.
- Use exports for board reports, government submissions, or your own archives.

> **Tip:** Run an export at the end of each cycle to keep a permanent record of all applications and outcomes.

## What's Next?

Once a student is accepted and enrolled through admissions, they automatically appear in your school's student roster — ready for class assignment, fee billing, and everything else EduSentrix offers.
