# Schemes of Work

Schemes of Work are the backbone of structured teaching. In EduSentrix, a scheme is a weekly plan that maps your curriculum standards to actual classroom lessons, activities, and assessments — giving every teacher a clear roadmap for the term.

## What Is a Scheme of Work?

A scheme breaks an entire term into week-by-week plans. Each week entry typically includes:

- **Week number and date range**
- **Strand / Sub-strand** from the curriculum (e.g., NaCCA standards)
- **Content standard and learning indicator** being targeted
- **Teaching and learning activities** for the week
- **Resources** needed (textbooks, manipulatives, digital tools)
- **Assessment method** — how you will check understanding

> **Tip:** Think of a scheme as the bridge between the national syllabus and your day-to-day lesson plans. Once your scheme is approved, you can generate individual lessons directly from it.

## Importing a Scheme

Teachers can create schemes manually or import them from existing files. EduSentrix supports three import formats:

- **PDF** — upload a scanned or digital PDF of a printed scheme
- **CSV** — a simple spreadsheet export with one row per week
- **Excel (.xlsx)** — a richer format that can include multiple sheets

### How to Import

1. Navigate to **Schemes** from the sidebar.
2. Click **"New Scheme"** and choose **Import**.
3. Select your file and map the columns to EduSentrix fields (week, topic, activities, etc.).
4. Review the preview table and click **Confirm Import**.

> **Tip:** For NaCCA-aligned schools, EduSentrix includes pre-mapped templates for the Ghana national curriculum. Select your subject and grade level, and the column mapping is done for you.

## Creating a Scheme with Leo AI

Not sure where to start? Leo, the built-in AI assistant, can help you draft a scheme in minutes.

1. Click **"New Scheme"** → **Generate with Leo**.
2. Choose the **subject, grade, and term**.
3. Leo suggests a weekly breakdown based on the curriculum, which you can edit freely.
4. Adjust activities, resources, or pacing, then save your draft.

Leo learns from your school's past schemes, so suggestions improve over time.

## The Review & Approval Workflow

Schemes go through a structured review process before they reach students:

1. **Draft** — The teacher builds or imports the scheme.
2. **Submitted for Review** — The teacher sends it to the review desk.
3. **Under Review** — A department head or academic coordinator reviews content, pacing, and alignment.
4. **Approved / Returned** — The reviewer either approves the scheme or returns it with feedback.
5. **Activated** — Once approved, an admin can activate the scheme for the current term.

### The Review Desk

The Review Desk is a central queue where reviewers see every scheme awaiting attention. From here they can:

- Open the full scheme in a side panel
- Add inline comments on specific weeks
- Approve, return for revision, or escalate to a senior reviewer
- Filter by subject, grade, or submission date

> **Tip:** Reviewers receive a notification each time a new scheme is submitted, so nothing slips through the cracks.

## Coverage Tracking

Once a scheme is activated, EduSentrix tracks how much of it has been delivered.

- **Coverage percentage** shows the proportion of weeks that have linked, completed lessons.
- **Behind-schedule alerts** flag weeks where no lesson activity has been recorded.
- **Visual timeline** highlights completed, in-progress, and upcoming weeks at a glance.

Coverage data rolls up into school-wide reports, giving administrators a bird's-eye view of curriculum delivery across all classes.

## Editing and Versioning

- You can edit a draft scheme at any time before submission.
- After approval, changes create a **new version** so the original remains on record.
- Version history lets you compare what changed between revisions.

## Best Practices

1. **Start early** — Import or draft your scheme before the term begins so reviewers have time.
2. **Be specific** — Vague activity descriptions make it harder for substitutes or reviewers to follow your plan.
3. **Leverage Leo** — Even experienced teachers save time by letting Leo generate a first draft they can refine.
4. **Link resources** — Attach or reference digital resources so everything is in one place.
5. **Check coverage weekly** — A quick glance at the coverage tracker helps you stay on pace.
