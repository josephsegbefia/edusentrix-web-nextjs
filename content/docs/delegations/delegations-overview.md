# Staff Delegation

Not every task needs to go through the school administrator. EduSentrix's delegation system lets admins grant specific staff members access to particular modules — so your team can share the workload without compromising security.

## What Is Delegation?

Delegation is the process of giving a staff member permission to access and operate within a specific module of EduSentrix.

- By default, only administrators have access to sensitive modules like Admissions, Finance, and Settings.
- With delegation, you can extend that access to trusted staff members on a per-module basis.
- Delegated staff can perform actions within the module as if they were an admin — but only within the modules they've been granted.

> **Tip:** Think of delegation as handing someone a specific key, not the entire keyring. They get exactly the access they need and nothing more.

## How Admins Create Delegations

Setting up a delegation takes just a few steps.

- Go to **Settings → Delegations** and click **New Delegation**.
- Select the **staff member** you want to delegate to.
- Choose one or more **modules** to grant access to.
- Optionally set an **expiry date** — the delegation will automatically revoke after this date.
- Save the delegation. The staff member will be notified of their new access.

## Choosing Modules to Delegate

You can delegate access to a wide range of modules, including:

- **Admissions** — review applications, update statuses, manage cycles.
- **Finance** — view fee records, process payments, generate reports.
- **Attendance** — mark and review student attendance.
- **Examinations** — manage exams, enter scores, generate reports.
- **Student Records** — view and update student profiles.
- **Promotions** — manage promotion cycles and decisions.

> **Tip:** Start with a narrow delegation and expand later if needed. It's easier to add access than to clean up after over-sharing.

## Activity Logging

Every action taken by a delegated staff member is tracked.

- EduSentrix maintains a detailed **activity log** showing what was accessed, what was changed, and when.
- Admins can review the log from **Settings → Delegations → Activity Log**.
- Logs include the staff member's name, the module, the specific action, and a timestamp.
- This ensures full accountability and makes auditing simple.

## Delegation Expiry and Management

Delegations don't have to be permanent.

- Set an **expiry date** when creating a delegation to automatically revoke access after a certain period.
- This is ideal for temporary assignments — for example, giving a teacher admissions access only during the enrollment window.
- You can also **manually revoke** a delegation at any time from the delegation management screen.
- Expired and revoked delegations are archived for reference.

> **Tip:** Review your active delegations at the start of each term. Remove any that are no longer needed to keep your access controls tight.

## Best Practices

- Delegate to staff members who are trained on the module — access without understanding can lead to mistakes.
- Use expiry dates for seasonal responsibilities like admissions or exam grading.
- Check the activity log regularly, especially after delegating access to sensitive modules like Finance.
- Communicate with your team about what they've been delegated so expectations are clear.
