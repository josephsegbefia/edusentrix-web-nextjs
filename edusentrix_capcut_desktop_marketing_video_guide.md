# EduSentrix Marketing Video Editing Guide for CapCut Desktop on Mac

This practical guide shows how to edit EduSentrix marketing videos in **CapCut Desktop for Mac** after recording clean walkthroughs with **Screen Studio**.

The goal is to create polished marketing videos that help convince schools to pilot the EduSentrix platform.

---

## Part 1: Prepare Your Files Before Opening CapCut

Create one folder on your Mac called:

```text
EduSentrix Marketing Video
```

Inside it, create these folders:

```text
EduSentrix Marketing Video
├── 01 Screen Studio Exports
├── 02 Logo & Brand Assets
├── 03 Voiceover
├── 04 Music
├── 05 Final Exports
```

Put your Screen Studio videos inside **01 Screen Studio Exports**.

Example file names:

```text
01_landing_intro.mp4
02_admin_dashboard.mp4
03_students_teachers_classes.mp4
04_teacher_workflow.mp4
05_teach_mode.mp4
06_fees_finance.mp4
07_parent_portal.mp4
08_reports_learn.mp4
09_final_cta.mp4
```

Also prepare:

- EduSentrix logo PNG
- Appsentrix logo if needed
- Any presenter intro clip
- Background music
- Your voiceover script

---

## Part 2: Create a New CapCut Project

1. Open **CapCut Desktop** on your Mac.
2. Click **Create project**.
3. Name the project clearly, for example:

```text
EduSentrix Main Pilot Video
```

4. Set the project aspect ratio to **16:9**.

Use **16:9** for the main school-pilot video because it works well for:

- YouTube
- Website landing page
- Laptop presentations
- Projector demos
- WhatsApp sharing
- School proposal meetings

Later, you can create a vertical **9:16** version for WhatsApp Status, TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

---

## Part 3: Import Your Screen Studio Clips

1. In CapCut, go to the **Media** section.
2. Click **Import**.
3. Select all exported Screen Studio clips.
4. Import your logo, music, presenter clip, and any extra visuals.

Once imported, drag the clips into the timeline in this order:

```text
1. Presenter intro or landing page intro
2. Admin dashboard
3. Students / teachers / classes
4. Teacher workflow
5. Teach Mode
6. Fees and finance
7. Parent portal
8. Reports and Learn
9. Final CTA
```

---

## Part 4: Arrange the Video Timeline

Your timeline should roughly follow this structure:

| Section | Approx. length | Content |
|---|---:|---|
| Intro | 8–12 sec | Presenter or landing page |
| Problem | 10–15 sec | “Schools use too many scattered tools” |
| Admin dashboard | 20–25 sec | School control center |
| Student/staff management | 20–25 sec | Records, classes, teachers |
| Teacher workflow | 30–40 sec | Lesson planning, notes, studio |
| Teach Mode | 20–25 sec | Strong visual moment |
| Fees/finance | 25–35 sec | Invoices, payments, balances |
| Parent portal | 15–25 sec | Parent visibility |
| Reports/Learn | 20–30 sec | Insights and learning companion |
| CTA | 8–12 sec | Pilot EduSentrix |

Aim for **2.5 to 3.5 minutes** for the main video.

---

## Part 5: Trim Mistakes and Remove Boring Parts

This is the most important beginner skill.

1. Click a clip in the timeline.
2. Move the playhead to where you want to cut.
3. Use **Split**.
4. Delete the bad or boring part.
5. Drag clips together so there are no gaps.

Remove:

- Login waiting time
- Loading screens
- Repeated mouse movement
- Empty pages
- Awkward pauses
- Errors
- Unfinished UI
- Anything that makes the app look unpolished

Your video should feel fast but not rushed.

A good rule:

> If nothing useful happens for 2 seconds, cut it.

## Part 6: Add the Voiceover

You have two options.

### Option A: Use CapCut Text-to-Speech

This is easier if you do not want to use your own voice.

Steps:

1. Go to **Text**.
2. Add a text box.
3. Paste a short section of your script.
4. Look for **Text to speech**.
5. Choose a professional female voice.
6. Generate the audio.
7. Place it on the timeline.
8. Repeat section by section.

Do not paste the whole 3-minute script at once. Break it into sections:

```text
Intro
Admin dashboard
Students and teachers
Teacher tools
Fees and finance
Parent portal
Reports and Learn
CTA
```

This makes timing easier.

### Option B: Generate Voiceover Elsewhere, Then Import

You can use tools like:

- ElevenLabs
- PlayHT
- CapCut Web
- Any other AI voice generator

Export the audio as MP3, then import it into CapCut.

This often gives you a more natural voice.

## Part 8: Match the Video Clips to the Voiceover

After adding the voiceover:

1. Play the video from the beginning.
2. Listen to the voiceover.
3. When the voice says **school administrators**, show the admin dashboard.
4. When the voice says **student records**, show students.
5. When the voice says **teachers**, show teacher dashboard.
6. When the voice says **fees**, show fees and finance.
7. When the voice says **parents**, show parent portal.
8. When the voice says **reports**, show reports.

Do not let the voiceover talk about one thing while the screen shows another thing.

That is what makes a video feel amateur.

---

## Part 9: Add Captions

Captions are very important because many people may watch the video without sound, especially on WhatsApp.

Steps:

1. Click the voiceover audio in your timeline.
2. Go to **Text** or **Captions**.
3. Choose **Auto captions**.
4. Select **English**.
5. Click **Create** or **Generate**.
6. Wait for CapCut to generate the captions.
7. Review the captions manually.

Caption style:

- Use clean white text.
- Add shadow or a dark background.
- Keep captions at the bottom.
- Use only 1–2 lines at a time.
- Do not cover important buttons or dashboard numbers.
- Correct words like **EduSentrix**, **Appsentrix**, **Teach Mode**, **JHS**, and **Ghanaian schools** manually.

---

## Part 10: Add Section Labels

Section labels help viewers understand the app quickly.

Add short text labels whenever the video enters a new area.

Examples:

```text
School Admin Dashboard
Student Records
Teacher Workspace
Teach Mode
Fees & Finance
Parent Portal
Reports & Analytics
EduSentrix Learn
```

How to do it:

1. Move the playhead to the start of a section.
2. Go to **Text**.
3. Add text.
4. Type the section label.
5. Place it at the top-left or bottom-left.
6. Keep it on screen for 3–5 seconds.
7. Apply a simple fade-in/fade-out animation.

Keep the label style consistent:

- Same font
- Same size
- Same position
- Same animation
- Same color style

For a premium look, avoid using too many different text styles.

---

## Part 11: Add Problem-Solution Text Overlays

These are different from captions. Captions follow the voice. Text overlays emphasize the selling points.

Add overlays like:

```text
No more scattered school records
Track fees and balances clearly
Support teachers with better tools
Keep parents informed
Generate reports faster
Pilot EduSentrix in your school
```

Use these sparingly. Maybe 5–7 overlays in the whole video.

Good placement:

- Top-left
- Lower-left
- Center for major moments only

Avoid putting text over important UI areas.

## Part 12: Add the EduSentrix Logo

Add your logo in two places.

### Intro

At the beginning, show:

```text
EduSentrix
Modern school management for Ghanaian schools
```

### End Screen

At the end, show:

```text
EduSentrix
Run your school smarter.

Pilot EduSentrix today.
Website: tryedusentrix.app
Phone / WhatsApp: [your number]
Email: [your email]
```

In CapCut:

1. Import your logo PNG.
2. Drag it above the video layer.
3. Resize it.
4. Place it in the corner or center.
5. Use fade-in and fade-out.

Do not leave the logo on the screen throughout the whole video unless it is small and subtle.

A constant large logo can make the video feel crowded.

---

## Part 13: Add Background Music

Use calm corporate/technology music.

In CapCut:

1. Go to **Audio**.
2. Choose music or import your own.
3. Drag the music under the whole video.
4. Lower the volume.

Recommended music volume:

```text
Voiceover: 100%
Background music: 8%–15%
```

If the music competes with the voiceover, it is too loud.

Use music mainly to make the video feel professional, not emotional.

---

## Part 14: Add Transitions

Use simple transitions only.

Good transitions:

- Fade
- Dissolve
- Slide
- Light blur

Avoid:

- Glitch effects
- Spinning transitions
- Flashy TikTok transitions
- Too many zoom effects
- Shaking effects

For school decision-makers, the video should feel:

- Clean
- Trustworthy
- Modern
- Calm
- Professional

Not noisy.

---

## Part 15: Add Zooms or Highlights Inside CapCut Only When Needed

Most of your zooms should already come from Screen Studio. But in CapCut, you can still add emphasis.

Use this only when:

- Dashboard numbers are too small
- A button or menu is important
- You want to draw attention to fees, reports, or Teach Mode

How:

1. Select a clip.
2. Use scale/position controls.
3. Add keyframes if needed.
4. Slowly zoom from 100% to maybe 115%.
5. Do not overdo it.

For UI demos, too much zooming makes viewers dizzy.

## Part 16: Add an AI Presenter Intro

For your main video, this is a good structure:

```text
0:00–0:08: AI presenter speaks
0:08–0:12: EduSentrix logo / landing page
0:12 onward: Screen recording walkthrough
```

The presenter should not talk for too long. Schools are mainly there to see the product.

Suggested presenter script:

> “Meet EduSentrix, a modern platform helping schools manage students, teachers, fees, lessons, parents, reports, and learning from one place.”

Then immediately move into the app.

In CapCut:

1. Place the presenter clip at the beginning.
2. Add a smooth fade into the Screen Studio recording.
3. Keep the intro short and professional.

---

## Part 17: Build the Video in Layers

Think of your CapCut timeline like this:

```text
Layer 5: Section labels and text overlays
Layer 4: Logo
Layer 3: Captions
Layer 2: Main Screen Studio video clips
Layer 1: Voiceover
Layer 0: Background music
```

This helps you stay organized.

Do not put everything on one track if you can avoid it.

---

## Part 18: Final Polish Checklist

Before exporting, watch the full video from beginning to end.

Check:

- Is the voice clear?
- Is the music too loud?
- Are captions accurate?
- Is **EduSentrix** spelled correctly everywhere?
- Are the screens matching the voiceover?
- Are any pages empty?
- Are there any loading errors?
- Are there awkward pauses?
- Is the CTA clear?
- Is the phone number/email correct?
- Is the total video under 3.5 minutes?
- Does the video look professional enough to send to a school owner?

---

## Part 19: Export Settings

For the main video, export as:

```text
Resolution: 1080p
Format: MP4
Frame rate: 30fps
Aspect ratio: 16:9
```

If your original Screen Studio recording is very sharp, you can export 4K too, but 1080p is usually enough for school outreach and WhatsApp.

Name your final file clearly:

```text
EduSentrix_Main_Pilot_Video_2026.mp4
```

Save it inside:

```text
05 Final Exports
```

## Simple Editing Order to Follow Every Time

Use this order so you do not get confused:

```text
1. Import all files
2. Arrange clips
3. Trim mistakes
4. Add voiceover
5. Match clips to voiceover
6. Add captions
7. Add section labels
8. Add text overlays
9. Add logo
10. Add music
11. Add transitions
12. Watch and fix
13. Export
14. Duplicate and make shorter versions
```

Before editing the full EduSentrix video, practice with only three clips:

1. Admin dashboard

Make a 45-second practice video with:

- Voiceover
- Captions
- Section labels
- Music

- CTA

Once you can do that, the full 3-minute video will be much easier.
