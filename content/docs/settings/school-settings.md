# School Settings

The Settings area is where you configure how EduSentrix works for your school. From timetable preferences to payment setup, everything is customisable to fit your school's unique needs.

## School Configuration

Set up the foundational details and rules for your school.

- **School profile** — update your school name, logo, address, contact details, and motto. This information appears on reports and parent-facing pages.
- **Academic year** — define the current academic year and its terms or semesters.
- **Timetable settings** — configure the number of periods per day, period duration, break times, and days of the week your school operates.
- **Attendance rules** — set the time window for marking attendance, choose between per-period or per-day tracking, and define what counts as late versus absent.

> **Tip:** Get your academic year and timetable settings right before the term starts — many other features depend on them.

## Feature Toggles

Not every school uses every feature. Toggle modules on or off to keep the interface clean and relevant.

- Enable or disable features like **Admissions**, **Store**, **Supply Programs**, **Meetings**, and more.
- Disabled features are hidden from the sidebar for all users — no confusion, no clutter.
- You can re-enable any feature at any time without losing data.

## Teacher Studio Toggles

Teacher Studio is the teacher's workspace within EduSentrix. Control what tools are available to teachers.

- Toggle access to **Lesson Notes**, **Schemes of Work**, **Question Bank**, and **Leo AI** for teachers.
- Enable or disable **report card comments** and **assessment entry** per your school's workflow.
- These toggles let you roll out features gradually — start with lesson notes, then add the question bank later.

## Notification Channel Settings

Choose how your school communicates with parents and staff.

- **In-app notifications** are always active and appear within EduSentrix.
- **Email** — enable email notifications for fee reminders, meeting invites, report cards, and more.
- **SMS** — reach parents who may not check email frequently. Configure your SMS provider credentials.
- **WhatsApp** — send notifications via WhatsApp for higher engagement. Connect your WhatsApp Business API.

> **Tip:** WhatsApp and SMS tend to have the highest read rates for parent communication in Ghana. Enable at least one of these alongside email.

## Curriculum Settings

Link your school to the curriculum framework you follow.

- Select from supported curricula like **NaCCA (Ghana)**, **Cambridge**, **IB**, or create a custom framework.
- Map your school's subjects to the curriculum's subject definitions.
- This connection powers scheme of work alignment, lesson note standards, and examination blueprints.

## Email Configuration

Customise the emails EduSentrix sends on your school's behalf.

- Set a **sender name** and **reply-to address** so emails look professional and parents know who they're from.
- Customise email templates for common notifications — fee reminders, admission updates, and meeting invites.
- Preview emails before saving changes.

## Payment Setup

Configure how your school collects fees and payments.

- Connect your **payment provider** (e.g. Paystack, Hubtel) to accept online payments from parents.
- Set your **currency** and any applicable tax or surcharge rules.
- Define **payment categories** — tuition, feeding, transport, PTA dues, etc.
- Enable or disable **partial payments** depending on your school's policy.

> **Tip:** Test your payment setup with a small amount before the billing cycle begins to make sure everything flows correctly.

## Best Practices

- Review your settings at the start of each academic year — update the year, check feature toggles, and refresh notification preferences.
- Keep your school profile up to date so reports and communications always reflect accurate information.
- Use feature toggles to simplify the experience for staff — only show what they actually need.
