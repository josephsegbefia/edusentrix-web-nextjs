# School Store

The EduSentrix School Store is a simple e-commerce feature built right into the platform. Schools can list products — uniforms, books, stationery, merchandise — and parents can browse and purchase them online. No separate website needed.

## Managing the Product Catalog

Build your store by adding the products your school offers.

- Go to **Store → Products** and click **Add Product**.
- Enter the product name, description, price, and upload one or more images.
- Organise products into **categories** (e.g. Uniforms, Books, Stationery, Accessories).
- Set stock quantities so parents can see availability at a glance.
- Mark products as **active** or **inactive** to control what's visible in the store.

> **Tip:** Use clear, high-quality photos and detailed descriptions. Parents are more likely to purchase when they can see exactly what they're getting.

## Pricing and Variants

Handle different sizes, colours, and configurations with ease.

- Add **variants** to a product — for example, a school shirt might come in sizes S, M, L, and XL.
- Set different prices per variant if needed.
- Track stock separately for each variant.

## How Parents Browse and Purchase

The store is accessible to parents through their EduSentrix dashboard.

- Parents navigate to the **Store** section from the sidebar.
- They can browse by category, search for specific items, or view featured products.
- Add items to a cart, select quantities and variants, then check out.
- Payment is processed through the school's configured payment provider (e.g. Paystack, Hubtel).
- Parents receive a confirmation notification after a successful purchase.

> **Tip:** Feature popular or seasonal items at the top of the store to drive purchases — back-to-school packs, exam materials, or spirit wear.

## Order Tracking and Fulfillment

Once an order is placed, your team manages fulfillment.

- View all incoming orders from **Store → Orders**.
- Each order shows the parent's name, items purchased, quantities, and payment status.
- Update the order status as you process it: **Pending → Processing → Ready for Pickup → Completed**.
- Parents can see real-time status updates on their orders.

## Notifications

Keep parents informed throughout the purchase journey.

- Order confirmation is sent immediately after payment.
- Status updates notify parents when their order is ready for pickup or has been completed.
- Low-stock alerts can notify administrators when a product is running low.

## Reporting

Track your store's performance over time.

- View sales summaries — total revenue, orders by product, and orders by date range.
- Export order data to **CSV** for bookkeeping or financial reporting.
- Use insights to decide which products to restock, discontinue, or promote.

> **Tip:** Review your store analytics at the end of each term. This helps you stock the right products and set competitive prices for the next term.

## Best Practices

- Keep your catalog up to date — remove sold-out items and add new products promptly.
- Respond to orders quickly. Parents appreciate fast turnaround, especially for essentials like uniforms.
- Use categories to keep the store organised. A tidy store is easier to browse.
