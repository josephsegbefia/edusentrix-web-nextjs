# Notifications

EduSentrix keeps everyone informed with a robust notification system. From fee reminders to meeting invites, notifications ensure that important updates reach the right people at the right time.

## How the Notification System Works

Notifications in EduSentrix are triggered automatically by events across the platform. When something important happens — a fee is due, an application is approved, a meeting is scheduled — the relevant people are notified.

- Notifications are generated by the system based on actions and schedules.
- Each notification targets the appropriate audience: parents, teachers, admins, or specific staff members.
- You don't need to manually send most notifications — EduSentrix handles the timing and delivery for you.

## Types of Notifications

The platform sends notifications for a wide range of events, including:

- **Fee reminders** — automated alerts when fees are due, overdue, or partially paid.
- **Admission updates** — notifications to parents when their application status changes (received, under review, accepted, rejected).
- **Meeting invites** — alerts when a teacher schedules a parent-teacher meeting, with a join link.
- **Exam and report updates** — notifications when report cards are published or exam schedules change.
- **Attendance alerts** — parents are notified if their child is marked absent.
- **Approval workflows** — staff members are alerted when something needs their review or approval.
- **Store orders** — parents receive order confirmations and status updates.
- **Supply program updates** — notifications when supplies are ready for collection.

> **Tip:** Parents appreciate timely notifications. Fee reminders sent a few days before the due date reduce late payments significantly.

## Notification Channels

EduSentrix delivers notifications through multiple channels to maximise reach.

- **In-app** — notifications appear in the bell icon on the EduSentrix dashboard. Always active and available to all users.
- **Email** — detailed notifications sent to the user's registered email address. Ideal for formal communications like report cards and admission decisions.

> **Tip:** Configure your school's preferred channels in **Settings → Notification Channels**. You can enable WhatsApp and SMS for even broader reach.

## Managing Notification Preferences

Users have some control over how they receive notifications.

- Access notification preferences from your **profile settings**.
- Choose which types of notifications you want to receive on each channel.
- Critical notifications (like fee deadlines and approval requests) cannot be fully muted to ensure nothing important is missed.

## Marking Notifications as Read

Stay on top of your alerts without being overwhelmed.

- Click on any notification to mark it as **read** and view the full details.
- Use the **Mark All as Read** button to clear your notification badge when you've caught up.
- Unread notifications are highlighted so you can quickly spot what's new.
- Access your full notification history from the notification panel — scroll back to find older alerts.

> **Tip:** Make it a habit to check your notifications at the start of each day. This keeps you aware of any overnight updates or urgent items.

## Notification Best Practices for Admins

- **Review your notification settings each term** to make sure the right channels are active.
- **Customise fee reminder timing** — send reminders a week before, a day before, and on the due date for best results.
- **Use multiple channels** — not all parents check email. Adding SMS or WhatsApp ensures your messages get through.
- **Monitor delivery** — if parents report not receiving notifications, check their contact details and channel settings.
